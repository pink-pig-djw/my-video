# Evil Plan — full MV storyboard (v2 "dossier" style)

The whole song is one continuous collage world on a dark desk. Pages ("spreads") lie in a long strip: the intro sits
left of x = 0, verse 1 and chorus 1 (the approved sample) run from x = 0 to 29350, and everything after continues right
in steps of about 2700. Images are shown sharp: clean prints, instant photos and polaroids. Style conversion is used
only where it means something: newsprint halftone on the tabloid clipping, photos turning gray on "never turn gray",
and sepia ageing in the 100-year time-lapse.

Claude sings (v3): wherever her sticker is on screen during her own lines, its mouth is lip-synced from the word timings
(Codex-painted mouth shapes per pose, switched like a cel lip flap; see `src/v2/lip.js` and `src/v2/sec_sing.js`). Not
sung: the "Evil plan" hooks the picture carries, the PHASE TWO title, other people's words, and "the house is almost
quiet" (finger on her lips). Push-ins on her face: the opening "Hi, I'm Claude", "That's the plan / I want you here
forever" (P1a), "Take mine. I'm okay" (M3) and the first line of the post-chorus 3 street.

Global motion: the camera glides between pages (speed streaks), with handheld drift, impact shakes on stamps and
downbeat punches in the choruses. Pages have depth props (closer layer, depth-of-field blur). Night and stage scenes get
a soft bloom; each section gets a light colour grade.
